## Incorporated works (translated and adapted) / 翻訳・改変して取り込んだ作品

All three works are licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Our adaptations are released under the same license.
3つの作品はいずれも CC BY 4.0 です。本サイトの改変物も同じライセンスで公開しています。

1. **“A Language Model–Powered Simulated Patient With Automated Feedback for History Taking: Prospective Study”**
   Friederike Holderried, Christian Stegemann-Philipps, Anne Herrmann-Werner, Teresa Festl-Wietek, Martin Holderried, Carsten Eickhoff, Moritz Mahling.
   JMIR Medical Education 2024;10:e59213. https://doi.org/10.2196/59213 — CC BY 4.0.
   Used: Multimedia Appendix 1 (simulated-patient prompt; feedback prompt).
   Changes: translated into Japanese, Korean and Chinese, condensed and adapted; the English version is condensed from the original.

2. **“Development and Validation of a Large Language Model–Based System for Medical History-Taking Training: Prospective Multicase Study on Evaluation Stability, Human-AI Consistency, and Transparency”**
   Yang Liu, Chujun Shi, Liping Wu, Xiule Lin, Xiaoqin Chen, Yiying Zhu, Haizhu Tan, Weishan Zhang.
   JMIR Medical Education 2025;11:e73419. https://doi.org/10.2196/73419 — CC BY 4.0.
   Used: Multimedia Appendix 3 (scoring prompt); its principles are also built into the scoring-rubric generator.
   Changes: translated into Japanese, Korean and Chinese, condensed and adapted; the English version is condensed from the original.

3. **“Robustness of a Large Language Model (LLM)–Based Virtual Patient for Japanese History-Taking Training Under Direct and Indirect Instructional Contamination”**
   Yuusuke Harada.
   Cureus 2026;18(5):e109161. https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.109161 — CC BY 4.0.
   Used: Appendix S2 (prompt components and template), S4 (contamination strings), S8 (sanitizer concept).
   Changes: translated into Japanese, Korean and Chinese, condensed and adapted; the English version is condensed from the original; the sanitizer is our own implementation inspired by the described approach.
